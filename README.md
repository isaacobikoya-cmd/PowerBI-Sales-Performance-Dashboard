# 📊 Opeyemi | Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project showcases an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard transforms raw sales, customer, and product data into meaningful business insights, helping stakeholders understand revenue performance, profitability, customer activity, and product performance.

The project covers the complete analytics workflow — from data cleaning and transformation to data modeling, DAX calculations, visualization, and dashboard design.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The dashboard was developed to:

- Analyze overall sales and profitability performance
- Identify the most profitable brands
- Determine the most purchased product color
- Analyze revenue performance across the available sales period
- Identify the worst-performing month based on customer activity
- Analyze profit contribution across different income levels
- Provide interactive filtering for deeper analysis

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

Before building the dashboard, the datasets were reviewed table by table and column by column to identify and resolve data quality issues.

The preparation process included:

- Checking for missing and null values
- Reviewing and correcting data types
- Standardizing inconsistent text values
- Validating date fields
- Reviewing numerical fields
- Removing unnecessary data
- Establishing relationships between tables

The final data model connects the **Products**, **Customers**, and **Sales** tables to support the analysis.

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

### Brand by Profit
Analyzes profitability across brands to identify the most profitable brand.

### Color by Total Purchase
Analyzes purchasing volume by product color to identify the most purchased color.

### Revenue Trend
Analyzes revenue performance across the available January–March 2023 sales period.

### Monthly Customer Activity
Compares customer activity across the available months to identify the worst-performing month.

### Income Level by Profit
Analyzes profit contribution across customer income levels.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Features

Two slicers were included to allow users to dynamically explore the dashboard:

- **Brand**
- **Income Level**

These filters allow users to drill into specific segments and understand how performance changes across different selections.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

Some of the key findings from the analysis include:

- **Apple** was among the leading brands by profitability.
- **White** was the most purchased product color, followed by Grey and Black.
- The available sales data covers **January–March 2023**.
- **February** recorded the lowest customer activity among the three months.
- **Medium-income customers** generated the largest share of profit at **36.93%**, followed by High-income customers at **32.35%** and Low-income customers at **30.61%**.
